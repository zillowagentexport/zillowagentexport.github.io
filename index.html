Zillow Export & Agent Export - Name Phone Email

Three things from every Zillow listing: the agent's name, their phone number, and a working email address. Run a search, press Start, get a clean list of the people behind the listings.

Verified, not guessedThe email is kept only when the phone on the agent's own profile matches the phone on the listing.
Apartment buildings tooA building is one listing and many apartments, each let by a different agent. Every contact at that address is kept.
Your data stays yoursResults live in your browser and in a Google Sheet in your own Drive. Emails leave from your own Gmail.

01What it does

You open a search on Zillow the way you normally would. The extension reads every listing on it and collects the address, the price, the brokerage, and the listing contact: name, phone and email. It then exports the list to a Google Sheet in your own Drive, to Excel or to CSV.

An optional sending engine, installed inside your own spreadsheet, writes to those agents from your own Gmail address, follows up on your schedule, and stops the moment someone replies.
